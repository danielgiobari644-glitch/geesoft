04About Me

Behind the Work

I work at the intersection of technology, design and creativity.

I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into something people can actually use — whether that means building software, developing a website, creating an online platform, designing a church announcement, producing a promotional graphic, or shaping a complete digital experience for an organization.

My goal is simple: take ideas, solve problems and build things that are useful, functional and visually compelling. I don't only write code — I take ideas from first sketch to finished product, combining technology, design and problem-solving at every step.

  • I take ideas from concept to launch
  • I design and build — one person, one vision
  • I build for people first: simple, useful, beautiful
